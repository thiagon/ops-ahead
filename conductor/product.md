# Product Definition

## Project Name

FIAP AIOps Challenge

## Description

An ML platform to anticipate incident volume and OLA breach risk for Locaweb operations.

## Problem Statement

Locaweb's ops team currently reacts to incidents instead of anticipating them, leading to SLA breaches and inefficient resource allocation. This project aims to shift operations from reactive to proactive incident management through predictive analytics.

## Target Users

- **FIAP evaluators**: Academic reviewers assessing the challenge deliverable across 4 sprints
- **Locaweb stakeholders**: Operations and management staff evaluating the solution's viability

## Key Goals

1. **Forecast incident volume (D+1, D+7)** — Predict how many incidents will occur in the next 1 and 7 days
2. **Identify OLA breach risk** — Flag incidents likely to violate OLA time limits before they breach
3. **Deliver explainable insights** — Provide interpretable ML outputs that support operational decision-making
