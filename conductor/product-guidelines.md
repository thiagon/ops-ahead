# Product Guidelines

## Voice and Tone

**Friendly and approachable** — Accessible language that balances technical depth with readability. Documentation and outputs should be understandable by both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Design Principles

1. **Data quality and rigor** — Prioritize correct data handling, validation, and reproducibility. Every analysis step should be traceable and verifiable.
2. **User safety and reliability** — Predictions should be trustworthy and clearly communicate uncertainty. Avoid overconfident outputs.
3. **Innovation (Inovador)** — Explore creative approaches to incident prediction and visualization. Push beyond conventional ITSM analytics.
